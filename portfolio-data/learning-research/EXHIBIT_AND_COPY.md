## Position in portfolio

- Put **MathPath** as a feature within Digital Products and a compact **Research & Learning** story inside *The Archive / Ways of Thinking*. It must support **Event Production** and **AI & Creative Experiments**, not compete with the playable games, stage photography, published music and EventFlow.

## Layout proposals
1. **Hero teaser (compact):** a single research question, minimal typography, elegant diagram with two perspectives (creator / attendee). This is an editorial diagram, NOT a fabricated result.
2. **Explore the question:** a 4-step visual flow: Study question → Interviews → Qualitative coding → Compare interpretations.
3. **Learning vignette:** Starting point / one-to-one support / goal achieved. No invented student photo, testimonial, exam score or number of students.
4. **Academic line:** GPA shown in About or CV after scale is verified; don't make "100% essay success" a stat tile.

## What we deliberately exclude from main showcase
- Inflated roles: Lead Lecturer, Academic Supervisor, Thesis Writer, Certified Tutor, Education Director.
- '100% success rate' without denominator or verifiable cohort.
- Claim that unpublished thesis findings are peer-reviewed or the study is finished.
- Substituting hypothetical instruction steps for the owner's real tutoring process.
- Sharing personally identifying transcript, a private Google Doc link, or participant quotes without separate permission.

## Final editing rules
Use focused, strategic truth: a portfolio need not contain every ordinary experience or caveat, but every public factual claim should be supportable. This is more convincing than exaggerated job titles.
