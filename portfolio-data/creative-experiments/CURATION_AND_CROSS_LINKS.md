## Editorial principle
The portfolio is an exhibition of **real outcomes and the thinking behind them**, not a list of AI model logos, prompt screenshots or claims of hand-written coding.

Design the experience around a specific artifact:
1. What was attempted?
2. What can a visitor actually inspect, play, listen to, or interact with?
3. What creative decisions and iteration can be substantiated?
4. What was the user's personal role?
5. What is verified versus historical/prototype/proposed?

The AI-assistance label should describe the workflow **without implying sole manual implementation, human-level code authorship or proven business outcomes**.

## Layout system — one coherent SHOWTIME
- **THE SHOWROOM:** one unified creative-world entrance; avoid artificial separate rooms based on titles.
- **THE EXPERIENCE:** a distinct interaction grammar for the actual material (Event Command, Listening Room, Playable Games, Creative Lab).
- **THE ARCHIVE:** all other sourced media and experiments, findable without forcing them into oversized hero stories.
- **EVIDENCE DRAWER:** show creator attribution, source link, proven status, version and whether the artifact is a working demo or concept. Never invent a working interface to fill absent media.
